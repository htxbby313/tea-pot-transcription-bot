# Privacy Policy — Tea Pot Transcription Bot

**Last Updated: September 26, 2026**

### 1. Audio Data Handling
Tea Pot processes voice audio streams in real-time RAM strictly to generate speech-to-text transcriptions. **Raw voice audio is never stored permanently or sold to third parties.**

### 2. Two-Party Transparency
Tea Pot plays an audible chime (*"Tea Pot Transcription is now active in this channel"*) and sends a notification in text chat whenever it enters a voice channel.

### 3. Tiered Auto-Purge
Under the Free and Pro tiers, transcription threads are automatically purged after 24 to 48 hours unless configured for permanent retention.

### 4. Data Deletion
Server owners maintain full control. Deleting a thread or kicking the bot removes all associated session data.

### 5. Contact
For questions, visit: https://github.com/htxbby313/tea-pot-transcription-bot

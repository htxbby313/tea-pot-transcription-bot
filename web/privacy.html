Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Audio Data Handling

Tea Pot receives voice audio streams in real-time memory strictly to perform speech-to-text transcription. Raw audio files and voice recordings are never stored permanently on our servers or sold to third parties. Audio chunks are processed ephemerally in RAM and immediately discarded once transcribed.

2. Transparency & Consent

To ensure full transparency and two-party consent compliance, Tea Pot plays an audible chime (*"Tea Pot Transcription is now active in this channel"*) whenever it joins a voice channel, accompanied by a text notification in the designated transcript channel.

3. Data Stored

We store only essential configuration metadata necessary for bot operations:

  • Server ID & designated transcript channel ID
  • Configured retention duration settings
  • Temporary thread tracking IDs for automated cleanup

4. Tiered Retention & Auto-Purge

Under the Free and Pro tiers, transcription threads are automatically deleted after 24 to 48 hours unless upgraded to a permanent retention tier.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Server administrators have full control over their transcript channels and threads. Deleting a thread or kicking the bot immediately terminates any active sessions and purge tracking.

6. Contact Us

For questions or privacy concerns, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/htxbby313/tea-pot-transcription-bot.

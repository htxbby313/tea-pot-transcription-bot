How to Set Up Tea Pot in Your Server

Follow these quick steps to invite the bot or run your own private instance.

1

Invite Tea Pot to your Discord Server

Click the invite button to authorize Tea Pot with Voice & Thread permissions.

Generate Invite Link
2

Join any Voice Channel & Summon

Hop into your voice channel and type the slash command:

/join
3

View Live Transcripts & Manage Retention

Tea Pot automatically creates #tea-pot-transcripts and opens a thread for your voice chat. Use /leave when done!

1

Download the Package & Configure Keys

Clone the repository and copy .env.example to .env with your bot token and STT API key:

DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN=your_token
OPENAI_API_KEY=your_openai_key
2

One-Click Run

Run start.bat on Windows or ./start.sh on Linux/Mac:

./start.sh # or double-click start.bat
TRUST, SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

We prioritize privacy, transparency, and two-party consent above all else.

🔒

Privacy Policy

1. Audio Stream Processing

Tea Pot processes voice audio streams in real-time memory strictly for the purpose of speech-to-text conversion. Raw audio recordings are never permanently stored on external servers or sold to third parties.

2. Transparency & Consent

To uphold two-party consent principles, Tea Pot always plays an audible voice alert upon entering a VC ("Tea Pot Transcription is now active") and posts a prominent disclaimer in text chat.

3. Tiered Retention & Auto-Purge

Under the Free and Pro tiers, transcription threads are automatically purged from the Discord server after 24 to 48 hours unless upgraded to the Premium retention tier.

4. Data Control & Deletion

Server administrators maintain full ownership and control of their transcript channels. Deleting a thread or kicking the bot immediately terminates any active session buffers.

📜

Terms of Service

1. Permitted Use

Tea Pot is designed for legitimate meeting note-taking, accessibility, gaming communication, and podcast documentation. Users may not use Tea Pot for unauthorized covert surveillance or unlawful wiretapping.

2. Discord Community Guidelines

All users must comply with the official Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Server owners are responsible for ensuring members in their guild are notified of active transcriptions.

3. Service Availability

While we strive for 99.9% uptime, transcription speed and availability may depend on Discord Voice gateway status and underlying Speech-to-Text API cloud services.

4. Subscription & Tiers

Tier upgrades grant enhanced retention windows, export capabilities, and priority queues. Subscriptions may be cancelled anytime with no cancellation fees.
